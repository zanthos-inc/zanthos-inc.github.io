Process

How an engagement works.

  1. Free 30-minute consult. Bring your top ticket categories; we tell you honestly what AI can and cannot handle for your support load.
  2. Scoped pilot on real tickets. We build against a slice of your actual support history so you can judge answer quality on your own data.
  3. Production rollout. Helpdesk integration, escalation rules, and monitoring — deployed to your cloud or ours.
  4. Tune and hand off. We review live performance with you, tighten the system, and leave your team able to run it.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Will the AI make things up when talking to our customers?

We build retrieval-first systems: the AI answers only from your approved docs, shows its sources, and hands off to a human when it isn't confident. That architecture exists specifically to reduce hallucination.

Do we have to replace our helpdesk software?

No. We integrate with the tools you already use — Zendesk, Intercom, HubSpot, Freshdesk, or a shared inbox — so your team keeps its existing workflow.

How long does it take to go live?

A scoped pilot on real traffic typically lands within a few weeks; production follows once you've seen the quality on your own tickets.

What happens to our customer data?

Your data stays in your accounts. We build on API providers with enterprise data-use terms — Anthropic and OpenAI don't train on API data by default — and can deploy inside your own cloud.

Buried in repetitive tickets?

Grab time directly on our calendar, or send a note — we enjoy difficult questions and reply promptly.
