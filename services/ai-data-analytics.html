Process

How an engagement works.

  1. Free 30-minute consult. Tell us the questions your team keeps asking about the business; we'll map them to your data sources.
  2. Pilot against real questions. We wire up one data source and test the system on questions you already know the answers to.
  3. Production rollout. Permissions, query verification, and delivery in the tools your team lives in.
  4. Extend. More data sources, scheduled reports, and alerts as trust in the system grows.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How do we know the AI's answers about our numbers are right?

Every answer comes with the exact query the AI ran, so it's verifiable, not a black box. We test against questions with known answers from your own data before anyone relies on it.

Can it write to or change our database?

No. Analytics access is read-only by design, with per-user permissions.

Do we need a data warehouse first?

Not necessarily. We work with whatever you have — Postgres, MySQL, SQL Server, spreadsheets, SaaS exports — and will tell you honestly if your data needs cleanup before AI adds value.

We already have dashboards. Why this?

Dashboards answer the questions someone predicted. This answers the question you have right now, without waiting for an analyst to build a new chart.

Got questions your data should be answering?

Grab time directly on our calendar, or send a note — we enjoy difficult questions and reply promptly.
