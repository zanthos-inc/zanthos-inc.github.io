FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Our documents are messy — scans, PDFs, emails, spreadsheets. Can you handle that?

Yes — messy input is the normal case. Modern AI models read scans, tables, and inconsistent formats far better than legacy OCR, and validation checks flag bad extractions for a person instead of letting them flow into your systems.

What if the AI gets a field wrong?

Every pipeline includes confidence scoring and a human review step for low-confidence extractions. You choose the threshold: fully automatic for routine documents, reviewed for anything unusual.

Does this replace our existing systems?

No — it feeds them. Extracted, validated data lands in the ERP, CRM, accounting, or database systems you already run.

Which workflows are the best starting point?

High-volume, repetitive ones: invoice intake, contract review, form processing, report drafting, and email-to-system data entry. We help you pick the highest-ROI starting point in the first call.

Grab time directly on our calendar, or send a note — we enjoy difficult questions and reply promptly.
